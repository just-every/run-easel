### RL-101 — ACCESS-002: SSO and SCIM

**Date:** 2026-10-28  
**Ruled by:** ct  
**Governs:** ACCESS-002  
**Outcome:** adopt with rider

**Question:** May enterprise provisioning create ordinary members without claiming privileged native grants?

**Default proposed/adopted:** Explicit existing-account associations, ordinary SCIM membership, no auto-link/JIT privilege, current native grants and immediate revocation.

**Clause outcome:** Require explicit configured identity associations and native privilege grants, limit SCIM to ordinary membership, revoke deprovisioned live authority promptly and preserve historical attribution without restoring credentials.

**Placement:** ACCESS-002 owns identity association and cites ACCESS-001 for current grants; preserve every named IdP, SAML/OIDC, group-change and history journey.

**Date:** 2026-10-28

**Outcome:** adopt with rider

**Question:** May aggregate bank observations be limited to current company-wide finance readers?
