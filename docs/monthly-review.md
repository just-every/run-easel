Each month, replace the plan with what actually happened, explain the differences, and publish the result.

## Close a month

First [move in that month's actuals](/docs/move-in). Then, on the **Review** tab, finance presses **Close Sep 2026**, named for whichever month is next. Easel keeps the forecast as it stood, then replaces the month's planned amounts with the accepted actuals.

## Explain the variances

**Variances** lists each line and department with its actual, budget and variance, marked favourable or unfavourable, and the prior forecast. Open a row to see:

- a draft explanation that cites the accepted rows; anything it cannot cite is marked as a hypothesis;
- the **Accepted rows** behind the actual: file, line, date, description, reference and amount;
- the explanation form.

The department, or finance, writes the explanation and presses **Save explanation**, then **Confirm explanation**.

## Publish the review pack

Finance presses **Start the Aug 2026 pack**, named for the month you chose, records decisions and actions with an owner and a due date, and presses **Publish pack**. A published pack keeps its numbers, explanations and decisions. Everyone, viewers included, can download it with **Download pack (HTML)** or **Download pack (CSV)**. It carries no individual pay detail. Finance marks actions done as they happen without changing the pack.

## Department statements

After close, each active department contributor receives a statement for that department through the configured email provider. **Department statements** on Review shows captured actual/budget values, top variances, every supporting accepted row, current open tasks and explanations to confirm. Confirm explanations here or in Variances; download the scoped Excel statement from the same card. Email links require sign-in and current department access. Finance can inspect each department and retry pending email.

Mail status distinguishes provider acceptance, pending/refused mail, uncertain outcomes and local suppression. Acceptance does not establish mailbox delivery. Local development suppresses mail. An accepted or uncertain attempt is never blindly resent.

Finance can give a pack action a department when recording it, or assign an older published action using **Department for action**. That classification is separate metadata: it does not rewrite the published pack. Only explicitly tagged open actions appear in department statements. Unclassified actions stay in the company pack with a finance reminder; Easel never guesses their department from the owner's name.
