# Reviewing committed spend

Finance connects Ramp, Brex, Purse and Coaster under Settings. Use a Ramp credential restricted to transaction reads, a company-wide Brex credential restricted to `transactions.card.readonly`, a Purse token with `read-purchase-orders` for selected suppliers, and a Coaster key restricted to `claim-reports:read`. The installation owner configures `PURSE_ORIGIN` and `COASTER_ORIGIN` as HTTPS origins. Select only the sources needed for the review; a Coaster report does not require a Ramp or Purse connection. Easel makes read requests only, refuses redirects, and never initiates purchases or payments. Credentials are sealed and excluded from both open exports and owner archives. A restored installation must reconnect.

In Review, open Committed spend. Choose an accounting month, the desired providers, explicit Purse PO IDs and any Coaster final report IDs. Ramp cleared transactions and Brex settled transactions are selected by that accounting period. A recurring allocation reads the complete bounded source response and requires explicit allocation of every returned row. Pending and declined cards do not count as actual spend. Purse and Coaster use individual scoped record reads; no listing permission is assumed. Draft or unapproved reports refuse the read.

Choose a dedicated one-off expense in the working company forecast, or enter an allocation end month and choose a recurring expense active throughout the selected months. For a recurring review, enter every source amount for every reviewed month, including explicit zeroes. Allocations must retain the source direction and sum exactly to each source total; Easel never guesses an allocation or leaves a balance unassigned. The accounting period chosen for an allocation may differ from its settlement date, which remains visible as source provenance. All reviewed months change atomically and unreviewed months retain the original recurring assumption. A later review must include every previously reviewed month, and closed months refuse correction. These expense allocations do not prove cash settlement in the chosen service month. Its line, department and month become the explicit mapping for every reviewed source. Select a PO for each card that pays that obligation; leave unrelated cards separate. A linked card counts once as actual and reduces that PO's open commitment. A signed Ramp refund reduces actual and restores that commitment. Net linked payments below zero or above the PO amount refuse acceptance. A closed PO releases exactly its unpaid balance while preserving paid actual.

A Purse bill becomes actual only when its payment status is paid and carries the authenticated Pantry return timestamp. The status is Purse's last authenticated observation, so refresh Purse for later changes. An unknown bill status is never treated as payment evidence. If the same payment appears as both a paid bill and a card transaction, choose its source under Same actual as and enter the matched part when their totals differ. Both representations must belong to the same PO, come from different providers, and retain an independent counted actual. Only the entered overlap is removed; any remainder stays additional provider actual and reduces a linked PO by that remainder. Several rows may match one independent actual, but their combined matched parts cannot exceed its amount. A row that declares duplicate parts cannot itself receive them, preventing chains and circular matches. A blank matched part requires equal whole amounts. Recurring matches explicitly name each reviewed month. The HTTP mapping can supply several `duplicateMatches` with a source and decimal amount, or an `amounts` map for recurring periods. Matching across periods, reversed directions, reused payment capacity and amounts exceeding either representation refuse.

Coaster reads the final approved report with its revision, snapshot fingerprint and final approval. Approved and batched reports remain commitments; reimbursed reports become actual without adding the amount twice. A report's externally recorded reimbursement remains labelled external recording, never confirmed bank settlement. The reviewed report total must reconcile to its item amounts. Mixed report allocations require separate review outside this initial dedicated-cell slice.

When accepted ledger rows exist in the selected cell, review every row. Choose one equal source actual for a duplicate, or leave it as additional actual. An additional ledger payment can explicitly link to a PO. Confirm the complete ledger review before previewing. Use Matched part to split one provider payment across multiple ledger rows. Only the unmatched ledger remainder stays additional actual and may reduce a linked PO. The HTTP door also supports several distinct provider matches in one ledger row. A provider row with duplicate parts offers only its unmatched remainder for ledger matching; a fully duplicated row offers none. Matches must retain their direction and may never exceed either the source amount or the ledger row. An omitted part means the entire ledger row, subject to the same limits. Ledger rows, amounts and mappings enter the preview fingerprint, so a changed import or mapping requires another review. Spend acceptance never changes accepted ledger entries.

The preview reads the immutable approved budget and shows the line, department, month, budget, committed, actual, remaining and released balance. Remaining equals budget minus actual minus open commitment. Acceptance replaces each selected expense month with actual plus commitment, without another expense or ledger posting. Re-reading the same source updates that assumption. A source assigned to another driver is refused. Mixed forecast cells, dimensioned cells, closed months, unsupported currencies, excess PO payments and incomplete provider reads are refused rather than guessed.

Accepted reviews retain their source figures after disconnect. Later forecast edits are marked as requiring review again. Disconnect deletes the local sealed credential and records that remote revocation is unsupported. Revoke the source credential in Ramp or Brex developer settings, Purse integration-token settings or Coaster API-key settings; Easel does not claim provider revocation.

This connector slice has network-edge fixture qualification only. Committed contracts need a Purse public read-only contract door; the documented integration currently exposes POs only. Bill.com, allocation across multiple lines or departments, mixed or dimensioned cells, non-card refunds, chargebacks and live provider qualification remain unfinished. Requested source scopes are operator instructions until a live provider grant is qualified. No provider credentials are provisioned by the example fixtures.

Ramp uses the structured settlement-currency `entity_amount` integer value; deprecated bare transaction amounts are never scaled or guessed. See [Ramp monetary values](https://docs.ramp.com/developer-api/v1/monetary-values). Ramp token revocation needs application client credentials, which this pasted-token slice does not store.

Brex uses its [settled card transactions endpoint](https://developer.brex.com/openapi/transactions_api/transactions/listprimarycardtransactions). Each bounded page is read with a fixed origin and an encoded cursor; transaction IDs must be distinct across pages. Purchase and fee types become positive expense magnitudes and refund types negative magnitudes, with the original signed integer retained. Card-account collections and rewards credits do not become expenses. Chargebacks refuse because their expense direction is not established by this slice. A regular-user Brex token may expose only that person’s transactions; company-wide scope remains an operator requirement until a real grant is qualified.
