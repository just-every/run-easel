# Reviewing committed spend

Finance connects Ramp, Purse and Coaster under Settings. Use a Ramp credential restricted to transaction reads, a Purse token with `read-purchase-orders` for selected suppliers, and a Coaster key restricted to `claim-reports:read`. The installation owner configures `PURSE_ORIGIN` and `COASTER_ORIGIN` as HTTPS origins. Select only the sources needed for the review; a Coaster report does not require a Ramp or Purse connection. Easel makes read requests only, refuses redirects, and never initiates purchases or payments. Credentials are sealed and excluded from both open exports and owner archives. A restored installation must reconnect.

In Review, open Committed spend. Choose an accounting month, the desired providers, explicit Purse PO IDs and any Coaster final report IDs. Ramp cleared transactions are selected by that accounting period. Pending and declined cards do not count as actual spend. Purse and Coaster use individual scoped record reads; no listing permission is assumed. Draft or unapproved reports refuse the read.

Choose a dedicated one-off expense in the working company forecast. Its line, department and month become the explicit mapping for every reviewed source. Select a PO for each card that pays that obligation; leave unrelated cards separate. A linked card counts once as actual and reduces that PO's open commitment. A signed Ramp refund reduces actual and restores that commitment. Net linked payments below zero or above the PO amount refuse acceptance. A closed PO releases exactly its unpaid balance while preserving paid actual.

A Purse bill becomes actual only when its payment status is paid and carries the authenticated Pantry return timestamp. The status is Purse's last authenticated observation, so refresh Purse for later changes. An unknown bill status is never treated as payment evidence. If the same payment appears as both a paid bill and a card transaction, choose its equal-value source under Same actual as. Both representations must belong to the same PO, come from different providers, and retain one independent counted actual. Split-value or circular matches refuse rather than guessing.

Coaster reads the final approved report with its revision, snapshot fingerprint and final approval. Approved and batched reports remain commitments; reimbursed reports become actual without adding the amount twice. A report's externally recorded reimbursement remains labelled external recording, never confirmed bank settlement. The reviewed report total must reconcile to its item amounts. Mixed report allocations require separate review outside this initial dedicated-cell slice.

When accepted ledger rows exist in the selected cell, review every row. Choose one equal source actual for a duplicate, or leave it as additional actual. An additional ledger payment can explicitly link to a PO. Confirm the complete ledger review before previewing. A source actual cannot be matched to two ledger rows. Ledger rows, amounts and mappings enter the preview fingerprint, so a changed import or mapping requires another review. Spend acceptance never changes accepted ledger entries.

The preview reads the immutable approved budget and shows the line, department, month, budget, committed, actual, remaining and released balance. Remaining equals budget minus actual minus open commitment. Acceptance replaces the selected expense assumption with actual plus commitment, without another expense or ledger posting. Re-reading the same source updates that assumption. A source assigned to another driver is refused. Mixed forecast cells, dimensioned cells, closed months, unsupported currencies, excess PO payments and incomplete provider reads are refused rather than guessed.

Accepted reviews retain their source figures after disconnect. Later forecast edits are marked as requiring review again. Disconnect deletes the local sealed credential and records that remote revocation is unsupported. Revoke the source credential in Ramp developer settings, Purse integration-token settings or Coaster API-key settings; Easel does not claim provider revocation.

This connector slice has network-edge fixture qualification only. Committed contracts, Brex, Bill.com, general recurring allocation, cross-period settlement attribution, other provider refunds, split-value duplicate matches and live provider qualification remain unfinished. Requested source scopes are operator instructions until a live provider grant is qualified. No provider credentials are provisioned by the example fixtures.

Ramp uses the structured settlement-currency `entity_amount` integer value; deprecated bare transaction amounts are never scaled or guessed. See [Ramp monetary values](https://docs.ramp.com/developer-api/v1/monetary-values). Ramp token revocation needs application client credentials, which this pasted-token slice does not store.
