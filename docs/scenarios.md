## Branch a scenario

On the **Scenarios** tab, finance chooses a plan under **Branch from**, names the copy, and presses **Branch scenario**. Change its assumptions on the **Plan** tab, for example move a hire's start month to model slower hiring.

A scenario can replace a working plan's assumptions with **Use as …**, after a confirmation, or be deleted.

## Compare

Under **Compare**, choose two plans in **From** and **To**. Easel draws both closing cash lines, then lists month by month:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Profit (each plan) | Profit for the month in each plan, and the difference |
| Cash (each plan) | Closing cash for the month in each plan, and the difference |

Below the table, **Changed assumptions** names every driver, position or expense that differs, and **Changed cells** lists every amount that moved.

## Proposals

A proposal is a copy of a working plan bound to that plan's exact revision. Anyone who plans can start one with **Start a proposal**, change it on the **Plan** tab and press **Submit for approval**. Finance compares it, then either presses **Promote revision n**, which applies that exact revision to the plan, or declines it with a reason. If the plan changed in the meantime, nothing is applied.
