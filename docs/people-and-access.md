Easel decides on the server what each person can see and change, whatever screen or route they use.

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Finance | Plan everything, move in files, run the budget cycle, close months, publish packs and manage people |
| Contributor | Revise, comment on and submit the budget for their own departments, explain their variances, and start proposals |
| Viewer | Read the overview, the budget cycle, scenarios, reviews and published packs |

The owner who finished setup is finance. Someone who signs in before finance adds them sees only "Waiting for finance".

## Private pay

Individual positions and salaries require **Pay detail** permission as well as access to the department. The owner has this permission; other finance members and contributors need an explicit grant. The owner opens **Settings → People** and presses **Grant pay detail** beside the person. A contributor with the grant can see and edit positions only in departments they are allowed to plan. Making someone a contributor alone does not grant pay detail.

People without the grant see permitted aggregate amounts with individual pay removed. Viewers never see individual pay, and published packs carry none. The owner can revoke the grant under **Settings → People**.

## Activity

The **Activity** tab, for finance, lists who changed what, when, and the plan revision before and after.
