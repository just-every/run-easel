The first time finance opens Easel, it asks for the shape of the plan. Everything here can be changed later under **Settings**.

## Set up your plan

| Field | What it means |
|---|---|
| Company name | The name shown at the top of every screen. |
| Reporting currency | One currency for the whole plan, `AUD` by default. Easel does no currency conversion. |
| Fiscal year starts in month | The month your financial year begins, July by default. |
| First plan month | The first month of the fiscal year you are budgeting. The plan runs twelve months from it. |

Press **Create the plan**.

## Departments

Under **Settings → Departments**, add each department that owns numbers, for example Sales, Engineering and Marketing. Tick **Shared costs department (allocated to others)** for a cost centre such as Operations whose costs you spread across the others; see [shared costs](/docs/build-the-plan#shared-costs).

## Planning lines

Planning lines are the rows of your profit and loss. Each one is **Revenue**, **Cost of sales** or **Operating expenses**. Mark the line salaries land on as a **Payroll line**: payroll is paid in its own month, and its detail stays private to finance and the department it belongs to.

## People

Under **Settings → People**, add everyone who plans with you, with a role:

- **Finance** plans everything and approves.
- **Contributor** revises and submits the budget for the departments you choose for them.
- **Viewer** reads the reports and published review packs.

**Send sign-in invitation** emails a person a sign-in link. More in [People and access](/docs/people-and-access).

## Account mappings

When you [move in actuals](/docs/move-in), each account in your general ledger is mapped to a planning line, to the cash balance, or excluded. The mappings are listed under **Settings → Account mappings**, where you can change them too.
